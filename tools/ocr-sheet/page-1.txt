Harbour Street Community Garden
Notice to plot holders, 14 March 2026
Dear members,
The garden opens for the season on Saturday, April 4, at 9:00 a.m. Keys can be collected from the tool shed between 9 and noon; please bring the receipt for your 2026 fee. Plots that are not claimed by April 25 will be offered to the 31 households on the waiting list, so let us know early if you need more time.
Water is available from the two taps near the north fence. To keep our bill below last year's ($612.40), we ask everyone to water before 8 a.m. or after 7 p.m., to use a can rather than a hose where possible, and to report leaks at once. The rain barrels hold about 900 litres in total and fill quickly in a wet spring.
Our first work party is on Sunday, April 12. We will turn the compost, repair the raised beds along the path and spread 6 cubic metres of wood chips. Gloves, rakes and wheelbarrows are provided. Children are welcome, but must stay with an adult near the pond.
Questions? Write to the committee at plots@harbourstreet.example.org, call 613-555-0147 (evenings only), or visit https://harbourstreet.example.org/news for updates. Minutes of the February 19 meeting are posted on the board by the gate.
